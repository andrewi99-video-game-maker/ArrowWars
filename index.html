Arrow Brawl

Platform archery brawl. Host a party and share the code, or join a friend’s party. The party leader controls the map, power-ups, chests and more.

Desktop: A/D move, W or space jumps, S drops, mouse aims, hold click to charge and release to shoot, right-click or F bashes, Q switches special arrows. Mobile: left thumb moves (flick up to jump, down to drop). Right thumb: hold and drag to aim and charge, let go to shoot, tap to bash. Tap the arrow list at the bottom to switch special arrows.
