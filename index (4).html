Arrow Brawl

A platform fighter. Shoot arrows, bash rivals off the ledges, and grab power-ups. Everyone in the same room code plays together.

Desktop: A/D move, W or space jumps (twice), S drops through, mouse aims, hold click to charge a shot and release to fire, right-click or F bashes. Mobile: left thumb moves, flick up to jump, down to drop. Right thumb: hold and drag to aim and charge, let go to shoot; a quick tap bashes.
